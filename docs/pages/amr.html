About this module

Retrieval of Acinetobacter baumannii genomes by AMR. Pick up to five genes, choose ANY or ALL, and export the accession list — ready for datasets download genome accession --inputfile list.txt.

Source

Gene presence/absence derived from Shelenkov et al.'s A. baumannii genomic dataset (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19186935).

How to use it

  1. Click a chip or use All Genes to find a specific gene
  2. Add up to 5 genes
  3. Switch between Match ANY and Match ALL
  4. Click 🧬 Accessions to download
  5. TSV / CSV / JSON exports include country, source, and year per genome

Data coverage

  • 59,160 A. baumannii genomes in the collection
  • 35 curated AMR genes

Full data download

Complete presence matrix: amr_all.tsv.gz (via Downloads).
